Privacy Policy

Arcade of Focus Games

Campbell Skye Inc. · Effective September 16, 2026

The short version: Arcade of Focus Games does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything the app records — scores, streaks, settings, your Focus Score — stays on your device (and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account). There are no ads, no third‑party analytics, and no tracking.

Who we are

Arcade of Focus Games (“the app”) is developed and published by Campbell Skye Inc. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. It applies to the iOS app, its home‑screen widgets, and its Apple Watch companion.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the app, and the app contains no third‑party software development kits (SDKs) for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or attribution. We never receive your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, or gameplay activity.

Optional Apple services you control

Some features rely on services provided by Apple. Each is optional and governed by your iOS settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive data from any of them.

  • iCloud sync. If iCloud is enabled for the app, your progress and settings are stored in the iCloud Key‑Value store of your own Apple Account so they follow you across your devices. This data is stored by Apple, not by us. You can turn it off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, scores you post to leaderboards and achievements you unlock are sent to Apple’s Game Center service and shown under your Game Center nickname. Leaderboard visibility follows your Game Center privacy settings.
  • Health (Mindful Minutes). If you turn on the Health option, the app writes your completed focus sessions to the Health app as Mindful Minutes. This is off by default. The app only writes this data; it never reads Health data, and Health data never leaves your device through the app. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Sign in with Apple. You may optionally use Sign in with Apple to personalize your in‑app profile. The app keeps the resulting name and account identifier on your device only; nothing is sent to us.
  • In‑app purchases and subscriptions. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us only with anonymous transaction information needed to unlock what you bought.
  • Notifications. Daily‑challenge reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a push‑notification server.
  • Siri Shortcuts and widgets. These read the same on‑device data described above so they can launch games and show your progress.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to review or delete beyond removing the app or its iCloud data.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your progress by deleting the app; if iCloud sync is on, also remove the app’s data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Disconnect Game Center, Health, or iCloud for the app at any time in iOS Settings without losing local play.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently — for example, if we add an online feature — we will update this page, change the effective date above, and update the app’s privacy details on the App Store before that feature ships.
