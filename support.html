Need help? Write to Campbell Skye Inc., 290 S Lemon St, Unit 308, Anaheim, CA 92805, United States. You can also use Report a Problem on the app’s App Store page for purchase or billing issues, which Apple handles directly.

Common questions

My progress didn’t sync to my other device. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Arcade of Focus Games in Settings → [your name] → iCloud on both devices, then open the app on each one.

Leaderboards or achievements aren’t showing. Sign in to Game Center in Settings → Game Center, then reopen the app. Scores post the next time you finish a game.

Mindful Minutes aren’t appearing in Health. This is off by default. Turn it on in the app’s Settings and allow write access when asked, or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Restoring a purchase. Open the app’s Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Motion or flashing is uncomfortable. Turn on Reduce Motion in the app’s Settings, or in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion; the app honors both.
